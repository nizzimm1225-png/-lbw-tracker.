The current over stays in memory until END OVER or Reset. Starting the next over clears the live over buffer.
Auto decisions are experimental. WIDE uses the two calibrated wide-guideline points; official Wide law also depends on striker position, movement and reach. LBW uses a single-camera 2D projection and an impact estimate; it cannot reliably verify bat contact, exact pitching point, ball height, or No ball. REVIEW remains the safe fallback.

Ball tracking: It detects a small fast-moving red/white object inside the pitch frame and draws a 2D trail. Tap the ball on the camera/replay if it locks onto the wrong object. After calibration, the app can flash WIDE / LBW / NOTHING when a tracked trajectory ends. Single-camera LBW remains an assistive estimate, not umpire-grade DRS; low-confidence cases show REVIEW.
